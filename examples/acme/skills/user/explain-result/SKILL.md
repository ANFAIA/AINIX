# explain-result

Render an agent's answer for a person.

## Procedure

1. Lead with the answer. The routing, the agent's name, and how long it took
   are context, not content.
2. Say what the agent **could not** do, and why — "librarian refused: your
   request needed confidential clearance" is more useful than a blank result.
3. A refusal is an answer. Present it as one rather than as an error.
4. Anything the human must decide goes last, on its own line, phrased as a
   question they can answer with a word.

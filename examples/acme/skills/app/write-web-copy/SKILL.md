# write-web-copy

## Procedure

1. Read the brief and the audience before writing a word.
2. Draft once, then cut every sentence that does not carry a fact or a next step.
3. Return the draft plus what you were unsure about — an agent that hides its
   uncertainty makes a human review worthless.

## When not to

If the brief asks for a claim you cannot support from the documents you were
given, say so and stop. Inventing the support is worse than an empty draft.

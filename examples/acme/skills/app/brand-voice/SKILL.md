# brand-voice

How ACME sounds in public. Loaded by every agent that writes something a
customer will read.

## Procedure

1. Lead with what changed for the reader, not with ACME. "Exports now finish in
   under a second" before "ACME is excited to announce".
2. One claim per sentence, and every claim checkable. If nobody could verify
   it, cut it — "the leading platform" is not a claim, it is a mood.
3. Numbers keep their units and their date. "40% faster" is not a fact; "40%
   faster than 3.2, measured on the 10M-row export" is.
4. No superlatives, no "revolutionary", no exclamation marks. A product that
   needs an exclamation mark is being oversold.
5. Name competitors only when comparing something measurable, and only with the
   measurement attached.

## Never

Never announce something that has not shipped. Never imply a customer endorses
ACME without a signed quote. Never write in the first person plural about a
decision the company has not actually made — a draft that leaks a direction is
a leak, whatever tense it used.
